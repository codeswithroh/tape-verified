## Creative direction
Polished editorial fintech: paper (#f1ece1) and ink (#0e0e0c), signal orange (#ff6a2b), gain #3fd68a, loss #ff5d5d. Instrument Serif display, Instrument Sans body, JetBrains Mono numerals (local @font-face). Real UI is the hero, framed and slowly pushed in, with short mono-label + serif-line callouts. Soft slides and crossfades. Avoid generic SaaS phrasing and abstract filler.
